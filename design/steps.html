One work item, step by step

Each step in detail

A skill is a short instruction file the engine hands the agent for that step. Shared skills ship with Service Line, one folder per step. A service can add its own, such as how to replay its traffic.

1IntakeYou

Goes in
Your request, in your words.
Happens
  • You state the outcome you want.
  • It is rewritten as acceptance criteria that can be checked.
  • The engine creates the work item and a separate checkout of the repository.
  • The workflow is the service default unless you pick another.
Skills
clarify-requestwrite-acceptance-criteria
Comes out
The work item with its criteria.
May touch
Nothing in the repository yet.

2ResearchImplementer

Goes in
The work item, the project instructions, and the lessons that apply.
Happens
  • Finds the code, tests and configuration involved.
  • Reads the conventions this repository follows.
  • Lists what is unknown and what could break.
Skills
map-the-coderead-project-rulesrecall-lessons
Comes out
Research notes with file references.
May touch
Read-only. No edits, no commands that change state.

3PlanImplementer

Goes in
Research notes and the service guide.
Happens
  • States the scope and what is out of scope.
  • Splits the work into small phases, each one checkable.
  • Names the check that proves each criterion.
  • Proposes a risk level with reasons.
Skills
write-plansplit-into-phaseschoose-checksassess-risk
Comes out
Plan v1.
May touch
Writes the plan only.

4Plan reviewVerifier

Goes in
The plan, the research notes and the criteria.
Happens
  • Looks for criteria with no check, and checks that would pass on broken code.
  • Looks for steps that are unsafe or outside the request.
  • Argues the risk level up if it is too low.
Skills
review-planchallenge-risk
Comes out
Findings attached to the plan.
May touch
Read-only. It cannot change the plan.

5ApprovalYou

Goes in
The plan, the reviewer's findings and the risk level.
Happens
  • You approve, ask for a revision, or reject.
  • You can raise or lower the risk level.
  • The engine binds the plan version, risk level, guide version and check list. Changing any of them later needs approval again.
Skills
None. This step is not run by an agent.
Comes out
A recorded approval.
May touch
—

6ImplementImplementer

Goes in
The approved plan, one phase at a time.
Happens
  • Builds one phase, following the repository's conventions.
  • Runs the fast checks itself before handing over.
  • Updates its notes and commits a checkpoint after each phase.
  • Stops and reports if the plan no longer fits the code.
Skills
implement-phasefollow-conventionswrite-testsself-checkcheckpointflag-scope-change
Comes out
Commits in the checkout and a session checkpoint.
May touch
Its own checkout only. It cannot see the hidden scenarios or touch the main branch.

7ChecksEngine

Goes in
The code snapshot and the bound check list.
Happens
  • Format and lint.
  • Build.
  • The guide's commands required for the approved risk level.
  • Records output, exit codes and file hashes.
Skills
None. This step is not run by an agent.
Comes out
Check results tied to the snapshot.
May touch
Runs commands in the checkout. No agent is involved, so a check cannot be talked into passing.

8VerifyVerifier

Goes in
The diff, the plan, the check results and the full service guide.
Happens
  • Starts the service in a test environment.
  • Walks the browser flows and replays traffic.
  • Runs scenarios the implementer never saw.
  • Reviews the diff for security and for changes nobody asked for.
  • Gives a verdict for each criterion, with evidence.
Skills
verify-criteriaexercise-browserreplay-trafficsecurity-reviewreview-diffwrite-findings
Comes out
Findings and captures.
May touch
Disposable test state only. It cannot edit the code it reviews.

9GateEngine

Goes in
The evidence bundle and the verifier's findings.
Happens
  • Confirms every required check and review is present and passed.
  • Confirms the evidence belongs to the current snapshot, plan and guide.
  • Routes to Ready, to repair, or to Needs attention.
Skills
None. This step is not run by an agent.
Comes out
A verdict in the run state.
May touch
—

↺RepairImplementer

Goes in
The failed check and its evidence.
Happens
  • Resets the checkout to the last checkpoint if its state is uncertain.
  • Finds the cause before changing anything.
  • Fixes inside the approved scope. A wider fix goes back to Plan.
  • The old evidence is discarded and Checks runs again.
Skills
diagnose-failurerepair-in-scopecheckpoint
Comes out
A new snapshot. At most two repairs.
May touch
Same limits as Implement.

10ReadyYou

Goes in
A summary of the change and its evidence.
Happens
  • The engine notifies you.
  • You review as deeply as the risk level requires.
  • You merge and deploy yourself.
  • What was learned is saved as candidates for the learning loop.
Skills
summarize-evidencecapture-lessons
Comes out
A reviewed change, and lesson candidates.
May touch
